Current work

Love Wins Learning Centre, Sembabule District.

The foundational learning-centre project of Rukundo Nesinga Initiative is located at Mirama Village, Lubaale Parish, Kyeera, Lwemiyaga County, Sembabule District, Uganda.

The centre represents the beginning of RNI’s vision to create safe, inclusive and engaging learning environments where children can access education, develop practical skills, explore their interests and grow into confident, self-reliant individuals.

RNI’s work is not restricted to the location of this learning centre. The initiative also seeks to engage other communities through outreach, sensitisation, partnerships and future projects.

Upcoming project

RNI Children’s Giveback Outreach — Lira

Status: Upcoming project
Date: 25 November 2026
Location: Lira City West, Uganda

The RNI Children’s Giveback Outreach is planned as an opportunity to share love, hope and encouragement with children through community engagement and practical support. The planned outreach includes food, scholastic materials, gifts and a day of happiness, interaction and care.

Until the outreach is completed, it is described as an upcoming project. After the event, this page will be updated with verified figures, approved photographs, resources distributed, partners, results and lessons learned.

Every child deserves to experience love, dignity and the opportunity to dream. Through this initiative, we are taking another step towards making that possible.

What we will report

Show the work, not just the numbers.

As RNI grows it will maintain reliable records and share practical updates that help supporters understand progress and challenges. Suggested measures include:

  • ✓Number of children and young people reached.
  • ✓Learners supported and learning resources distributed.
  • ✓Meals or food packages provided through outreach activities.
  • ✓Volunteers and community members engaged.
  • ✓Partners and institutions contributing.
  • ✓Projects completed against planned activities and objectives.

Any public reporting protects children’s privacy and dignity. Identifiable details or images are never published without appropriate permission.
