Get involved

There are many ways to help a child thrive.

Your time, skills, supplies, partnership or financial support can help RNI create more opportunities for children and young people in Uganda.

For organisations

Build a partnership with purpose.

We welcome conversations with potential partners whose goals align with education, child wellbeing and community development. A partnership should have a clear purpose, realistic commitments and agreed ways to report progress.

  • ✓Corporate social responsibility and employee volunteering.
  • ✓Foundation and institutional funding discussions.
  • ✓Child sponsorship through the proposed Sponsor a Dream concept.
  • ✓In-kind support such as approved learning materials and supplies.
