Privacy notice

Your trust and privacy matter.

This draft explains the intended approach to personal information. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed and adapted to Love Wins’ actual practices before launch.

Draft policy: this site prototype does not transmit or store form entries. Replace this draft with an approved privacy notice before enabling live forms, analytics, newsletters or donations.

Information we may collect

Depending on the services enabled, Love Wins may collect contact details, donation records, partnership enquiries and newsletter preferences. We should collect only information needed for a clear purpose.

How information may be used

  • Respond to questions and partnership requests.
  • Process donations and issue receipts through approved providers.
  • Send newsletters where a person has opted in.
  • Maintain financial, operational and legal records where required.

Payments

Live donations should be processed through a reputable payment provider. Love Wins should not store full payment card numbers or CVVs on its own website. Payment providers may process information under their own privacy terms.

Children’s information

We will avoid publishing sensitive information about children. Identifiable photographs, stories or personal details should only be used with appropriate permissions and in line with the child safeguarding policy.

Sharing and retention

Personal information should only be shared with authorised service providers or where legally required, with access limited to people who need it. Retention periods should be documented and information securely deleted when no longer needed, subject to applicable requirements.

Your choices and contact

Before launch, add a verified privacy contact, the organisation’s legal identity and registration details, the applicable complaint process, retention schedule, cookie/analytics details and procedures for access or correction requests.
