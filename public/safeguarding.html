Child safeguarding

Children’s safety comes before publicity.

Love Wins should protect the dignity, privacy and wellbeing of every child featured in its work or supported by its programmes.

This is a draft public commitment, not a substitute for a formally approved safeguarding policy, reporting process and staff training.

Our public commitment

We aim to treat every child with dignity and respect. The organisation should maintain clear procedures for preventing harm, raising concerns and responding to safeguarding incidents.

Photography and stories

  • Obtain informed permission from the appropriate parent or guardian and age-appropriate assent from the child where applicable.
  • Explain how photographs, names and stories will be used, and respect a refusal.
  • Do not publish a child’s address, school routine, contact details, sensitive history or other information that could place them at risk.
  • Avoid humiliating, exploitative or misleading portrayals and never make support conditional on being photographed.
  • Store original images and consent records securely with access restricted to authorised staff.

Reporting concerns

Before launch, publish a verified safeguarding contact and a safe reporting route. Staff, volunteers and partners should know how to report concerns and how urgent risks are escalated under applicable Ugandan requirements.

Partner and volunteer conduct

Define expectations for behaviour, supervision, appropriate communication, photography, site visits and background checks where applicable. Confirm the final policy with the organisation’s leadership and a qualified safeguarding adviser.
