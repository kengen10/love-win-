Programmes

Helping children learn, grow and thrive.

RNI’s work is organised around five programme areas that support children and young people from the classroom to the community.

Programme areas describe what RNI aims to do. Where an activity is already taking place it is labelled current work; where it is planned it is labelled upcoming; where it is still a concept it is labelled future vision. Activities develop as resources, facilities and partnerships grow.
01 · Education and learning

Love Wins Education and Learning

Improve access to quality, affordable and holistic education through Love Wins Schools, education support, back-to-school campaigns, child sponsorship and learning beyond the classroom.

02 · Youth empowerment

Youth Empowerment and Skills Development

Support young people to develop practical skills, confidence and pathways to opportunity: vocational and entrepreneurship training, digital literacy, mentorship, leadership and career guidance.

03 · Child welfare

Child and Youth Welfare Advocacy

Engage communities on issues affecting children’s safety and development, including child protection, school dropout, teenage pregnancy prevention, menstrual health, mental wellbeing and positive parenting.

04 · Talent and creativity

Talent, Creativity and Practical Development

Create opportunities to discover and develop abilities through music, arts, sports, agriculture, crafts, public speaking, leadership and technology.

05 · Community development

Community Development and Essential Services

Work with communities and partners on needs that affect children’s education and quality of life, such as safe water, sanitation, school improvements, environmental conservation and sustainable agriculture.

Our approach

Learning beyond the classroom.

Every child has unique abilities waiting to be discovered. The long-term Love Wins learning-centre model brings academic education together with practical learning, creativity, talent development and personal growth.

  • ✓Agriculture and environmental learning: school gardens, farming, food production and sustainability.
  • ✓Sports and physical development: teamwork, discipline and confidence.
  • ✓Music and creative arts: artistic expression and creative confidence.
  • ✓Craftsmanship and practical skills that may develop into future livelihoods.
  • ✓Technology and digital learning: digital literacy and engagement with technology.
  • ✓Leadership and personal development: communication, responsibility and decision-making.
  • ✓Health, hygiene and wellbeing: healthy habits and community awareness.
