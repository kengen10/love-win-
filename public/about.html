About us

We believe love can change the direction of a life.

Rukundo Nesinga Initiative (RNI), meaning “Love Wins,” is a community-focused initiative rooted in the belief that every child and young person deserves an opportunity to learn, grow, discover their talents and build a better future — regardless of their circumstances.

Our story

Built on a simple belief: every child matters.

RNI was inspired in part by the founder’s personal experience of barriers to education. It exists to create safe, welcoming and engaging learning environments for children in communities where access to education and essential resources is limited.

At the heart of the initiative is the Love Wins learning-centre model — an approach that looks beyond the classroom curriculum to nurture the whole child through academic learning, practical skills, creativity, talent development, confidence and personal growth.

The foundational project of RNI is Love Wins Learning Centre, located at Mirama Village, Lubaale Parish, Kyeera, Lwemiyaga County, Sembabule District, Uganda. It represents the beginning of RNI’s vision to create learning environments where children can access education, develop practical skills, explore their interests and grow into confident, self-reliant individuals.

RNI’s work is not restricted to the location of this learning centre. The initiative also seeks to engage other communities through outreach, sensitisation, partnerships and future projects.
Our name

“Rukundo Nesinga” means love wins.

Love Wins is more than a name; it is the philosophy that guides the initiative.

Love means showing up for children and young people, caring about their wellbeing, creating opportunities for their growth, and believing in their potential. RNI hopes that those who experience this love will one day extend it to others.

Love Wins is not simply about giving children an education. It is about creating an environment where children and young people are loved, equipped, empowered and prepared to build their own futures.

Vision and mission

What we are working towards.

Our vision

A society of opportunity for every child

To create a society where every child and young person, regardless of their background or circumstances, has access to quality education, opportunities for personal development, and the resources to become confident, skilled and self-reliant.

Our mission

Empowering children and young people

To empower children and young people through accessible and holistic education, practical skills development, talent nurturing, community sensitisation and strategic partnerships that promote their welfare, unlock their potential and create opportunities for sustainable livelihoods.

Our core values

Seven values behind the work.

These values guide how RNI works with children, families, communities and partners.

01 · Love and compassion

Every child deserves to feel loved

We believe every child deserves to feel loved, valued, respected and supported.

02 · Equal opportunity

Inclusion for every child

Every child and young person deserves an opportunity to learn, grow and succeed regardless of background.

03 · Empowerment

Self-reliance

We nurture knowledge, confidence and practical abilities so people can shape their own futures.

04 · Holistic development

The whole child

We value academic learning alongside creativity, sports, health, leadership and practical life skills.

05 · Community

Collaboration

We believe lasting change happens when families, communities, institutions and partners work together.

06 · Integrity

Accountability

We are committed to honesty, transparency and responsible stewardship of resources.

07 · Sustainability

Innovation

We encourage practical, lasting solutions that respond to community needs and can grow over time.

Our approach

Learning beyond the classroom.

RNI believes that every child has unique abilities waiting to be discovered. As resources, facilities and partnerships grow, the learning-centre model aims to bring academic education together with practical learning, creativity, talent development and personal growth.

  • ✓Agriculture and environmental learning: school gardens, food production and sustainability.
  • ✓Sports and physical development: teamwork, discipline and confidence.
  • ✓Music and creative arts: artistic expression and creative confidence.
  • ✓Craftsmanship and practical skills that may develop into future livelihoods.
  • ✓Technology and digital learning: digital literacy and engagement with technology.
  • ✓Leadership and personal development: communication, responsibility and decision-making.
  • ✓Health, hygiene and wellbeing: healthy habits and community awareness.

These areas will develop as resources, facilities and partnerships grow.

Geographical ambition

From one centre towards four regions.

RNI’s ambition is to grow beyond one school, district or community. Our long-term dream is to establish more Love Wins Schools and learning centres across all four regions of Uganda.
