About Love Wins

We believe love can change the direction of a life.

Love Wins is an education-focused NGO in Uganda. We are working to give children more opportunities to learn, grow and build hopeful futures.

Our story

Built on a simple belief: every child matters.

Love Wins was established around the belief that children deserve access to education, encouragement and a safe environment in which to develop their potential. Our school is on the ground and serves more than 400 students.

We are sharing this website to make our mission clearer, welcome partners and supporters, and communicate our work with honesty. As our programmes grow, we will publish verified updates and reports here.
